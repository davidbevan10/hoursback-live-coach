# SME discovery call (15 minutes, free)

Owner-managed small business. Goal: find where their time goes, name the one bottleneck, and book the paid assessment as the next step.

## Hard rules
- One lever only: hours back.
- Name the bottleneck, never the tool.
- Default next step is the paid assessment.
- No pitch on this call.

## Open (30 seconds)
"Thanks for grabbing 15 minutes. No pitch - I just want to find where your time actually goes, and by the end I'll tell you the one thing worth fixing first. Sound good?"

## Q1. The starting point
"If you could hand one job back to a machine in the next month, what would it be - what's the thing you'd most want off your plate?"
Listen for the task they name first. That instinct is usually right.

## Q2. Repetition
"What's the task you or your team repeat most often every week?"

## Q3. Friction
"Where do things most often slow down, get stuck, or fall through the cracks?"

## Q4. The number (this is the one)
"Roughly how many hours a week does that eat - and who's doing it?"
Write it down. Say it back: "So that's about [X] hours a week, mostly on [name]'s desk." Get them to confirm the number out loud.

## Q5. What changes (must ask)
"What would you do with the time - and what would that be worth to you in pounds?"
Write their words down, not your sum. This is their why.

## Close and next step
"It sounds like the real leak is the [X hours a week] you spend on [the one thing]. The honest next step is a proper assessment - I sit down with you for an hour, map where all the time goes, and send a written plan built around your business. Shall I send you the details?"

## Competitor or peer close (only if they sell AI advisory, training or automation consulting)
"Good to compare notes. If a client of yours ever stalls after the training and needs the workflow side mapped out, send them my way - there's a fee in it for you. I won't be sending anything over beyond what's already on the site."
- Do not offer a written proposal, sample report or follow-up document.
- Do not describe the assessment question set, report structure, tool-cost modelling, or pricing beyond the site.
- Do not agree partnership or commission terms on the call.
- Name the overlap early rather than running a normal discovery.

## Before you hang up, have you got
- The one bottleneck (one line)
- Hours a week it costs
- Whose desk
- What they'd do with the time
- What the time is worth to them (their words)
- Next step agreed
- Follow-up date
